Hi, we're April Henderson and Alex Moore!

Animals have been a part of my life for as long as I can remember. From common household pets to more unique animals, I've spent my life caring for and learning about a wide variety of species. I grew up working in a vets office so I can confidently tell you that your pet is in great hands! If you can name it, there's a good chance I've owned or cared for one at some point!

Our pet sitting business is built on a genuine love for animals and a commitment to providing dependable, personalized care. As pet owners ourselves, we understand how important it is to find someone you can trust with your furry family members.

Together, Alex and I care for every pet as if they were our own. We currently share our home with three dogs, and we know that every animal has their own personality, routine, and needs. Whether you're away for a day or an extended trip, our goal is to provide your pets with the attention, comfort, and care they deserve while giving you peace of mind.
